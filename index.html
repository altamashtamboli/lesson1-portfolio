01 — About

A builder who treats the interface as the product.

I work with founders and product teams who need a website or app surface that feels considered from the first screen to the last detail. Most of my time goes into layout, interaction, and the small decisions that make software feel finished.

I start from the content and the person using it. From there I shape a responsive layout, write semantic markup, and keep the styling system small enough that the next change is obvious.
